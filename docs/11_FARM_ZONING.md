# 11 — Farm Zoning — the 1-acre plan

> A worked example of what the 1-acre at Lokkanahalli looks like when
> divided into zones. Numbers are starting assumptions; ops adjusts them
> at the first kharif review.

> **Read alongside** `07_DATA_MODEL.md` (the canonical list of zones
> lives there) and `10_FARM_OPERATIONS.md` (how each zone is run).

## 1. The 1 acre as ten zones

Total area: **1 acre ≈ 4,047 m²**. Roads, irrigation channels, paths,
and the homestead footprint are deducted *after* the zones are sized;
the numbers below are net crop / use areas.

```
                       │       approx.   60 × 67 m        │
                       │   (perimeter hedge, all sides)   │
                       └──────────────────────────────────┘
```

| --- | -------------------------- | --------: | ------------: | ----------------------------------------- |

> **Why Z10 is "buffer".** A non-trivial portion of any small-hold
> agroforestry plot is *not* crop. We set Z10 generously so the headline
> 1-acre framing (`Z1`–`Z9`) does not overstate production. Z10 also
> includes mango / tamarind / neem as long-cycle shade trees and as a
> windbreak against the BRT edge.

## 2. Zone-by-zone plan

### Z1 — Greens & salad beds (`250 m²`)

- **Crops.** *Amaranth* (red + green), *fenugreek*, *coriander*,
  *spinach*, *lettuce* (winter), *mint* (perennial), *curry leaf*
  (perennial), *spring onion*.
- **Cadence.** 4 sowings/year. Rotation among 3 beds × 30-day cycles.
- **Yield target.** ~ 35 kg/week during peak; ~ 12 kg/week in deep
  winter / monsoon lulls.
- **Subscribers served.** Greens appear in *every* subscription tier
  each week. This is the basket backbone.

### Z2 — Root & tuber beds (`300 m²`)

- **Crops.** Carrot, beetroot, radish (winter), sweet potato
  (monsoon), elephant yam, cassava (perennial pockets).
- **Cadence.** 3 main plantings/year. Storage in shade for 2–3 weeks.
- **Yield target.** ~ 18 kg/week across the season.
- **Note.** Slightly raised beds for drainage. Mulch is critical here.

### Z3 — Millets & pulses (`800 m²`)

The heart of the seed farm.

- **Crops.** Ragi (kharif), jowar (rabi), foxtail millet (kharif),
  toor dal (kharif), horsegram (rabi), cowpea (kharif + summer).
- **Cadence.** Two main seasons. Inter-crop *cowpea-in-ragi* in
  kharif; *horsegram-after-jowar* in rabi.
- **Yield target.** ~ 35 kg ragi + 12 kg jowar + 8 kg pulses / season.
- **Why big.** Millets are the *identity crop* of the dry-deciduous
  belt. They store well, they ship well, they are what the subscriber's
  kitchen learns from. Most of the **millet-flour retail SKUs** in
  `04_BUSINESS_MODEL.md` §4 come from here.

### Z4 — Gau Shala pasture & fodder (`600 m²`)

- **Pasture.** Rotational grazing: 4 paddocks × 150 m² on a 7-day
  rotation. Set-stocking would compact the soil.
- **Fodder bank.** 200 m² of hybrid napier + 100 m² of sesbania + 100
  m² of lucerne (rabi only).
- **Trees on the perimeter.** 8–10 *subabul* (Leucaena) + *sesbania*
  shade trees.
- **Note.** This zone doubles as the buffer on the southern side, away
  from the BRT edge — cattle are not freely ranging into the reserve.

### Z5 — Pollinator & pest-trap strip (`100 m²`)

- **Crops.** Marigold (yellow + orange), sunflower, *tulsi*, *ajwain*,
  *dill*, *carrot-as-flower*, *coriander-as-flower*.
- **Function.** Hosts beneficials (syrphids, ladybird beetles, lacewings).
  Traps aphids / whiteflies away from Z1 and Z2.
- **Rule.** Never sprayed, even botanically. If marigolds fail, that's
  data; we don't reach for an alternative.

### Z6 — Apiary (`50 m²`)

- **Hives.** 4–6 colonies of *Apis cerana indica* (the Indian hive
  bee). Staggered re-queening.
- **Yield.** 8–14 kg of honey per hive per year, varying with
  monsoonal florals.
- **Subscribers served.** The "season's honey" SKU on `/shop`.
- **Safety.** A 4-metre green mesh + a clear flight path on the
  non-cafe side.

### Z7 — Homestead + stay + café (`350 m²`)

- **Existing.** Bamboo Cafe kitchen + seating. Dormer / family rooms
  (Phase 2 retrofits).
- **New build (Phase 2).** Two cottage rooms (30 m² each), 1 staff
  quarter, an outdoor dining deck.
- **Surface.** Stone + lime + bamboo; low-embodied-energy materials.

### Z8 — Cow shed + Homa corner (`150 m²`)

- **Cow shed.** 80 m² covered, sloped-floor, accessible to compost
  yard.
- **Homa kund.** A small concrete pad facing east, with a roof only
  over the offerings and the practitioner. East is non-negotiable.
- **Storage.** A lockable cabinet for the pyramid, samidhas, ghee jar.

### Z9 — Compost & nursery yard (`150 m²`)

- **Windrow.** 25 m² covered, sloped-floor, leachate sump.
- **Vermicompost bins.** 8 × 1 m³ bins, on the shaded side.
- **Nursery.** Raised trays + shade-cloth above; holds seedlings for 2
  weeks before transplant.

### Z10 — Buffer, trees, paths, water (`~1,297 m²`)

- **Tree lines.** Mango, tamarind, neem, *mahua*, *ficus*. Long-cycle
  shade + an eventual fruit contribution.
- **Rainwater trench.** A 60 m recharge trench along the lower
  boundary, lined with gravel.
- **Path.** A 1.5 m gravel-and-earth path running perimeter to compost
  yard to pack house.
- **Wildlife buffer.** Last 5 m before the property edge reserved for
  *unfarmed* native scrub — BRT wildlife corridor consideration.

## 4. Subscriber allocation logic

How many subscribers does this 1-acre actually serve?

We allocate per zone by **weekly dependable yield (kg)**, not by area.
A zone with low yields but high value (Z6 Apiary → honey) and a zone
with high yields but low weight-per-subscriber (Z1 Greens) are
weighted by what their harvest does in a basket.

```
Z1 weekly dependable yield: ~12 kg/week      (winter) – ~30 kg/week (peak)
Z2 weekly dependable yield: ~12 kg/week      (steady)
Z3 weekly dependable yield: ~5 kg/week       (in season)
Z4–Z9 produce inputs, not direct subscriber food
Z6 produces ~ 3 kg honey/month across 6 hives
Z8 produces milk, ghee inputs
Z10 produces occasional fruit (mango / tamarind)
```

Operationally: **1 acre (Z1–Z9 productive) = ~30 weekly baskets** in
peak season, **~12 baskets** in winter lull.

### Subscriptions tier → weight

| Tier            | Basket target (week) | Vegetables / grains / pulses                          |
| --------------- | -------------------: | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Half-plot       |               0.8 kg | 0.4 kg greens + 0.2 kg millets + 0.2 kg seasonal       |
| Plot            |               1.6 kg | 0.6 kg greens + 0.4 kg millets + 0.3 kg pulse/tuber    |
| Family-plot     |               3.0 kg | + 0.5 kg dairy-adjacent + occasional fruit              |
| Feast-plot      |               5.0 kg | + value-added (honey / pickle / soap)                  |

Thirty baskets of *Plot* tier per week × 1.6 kg = ~48 kg/week
utilised. That is a healthy ratio; the farm is producing for human
plates, not for inventory.

## 5. Capacity ceilings

- **Subscribers (Plot tier).** Hard ceiling ~ 30 in peak; ~ 12 in
  winter lull. The lull is solved by *pausing the cheapest tier*
  (one-month max; already part of `FR-1.6`).
- **Stay visitors.** Z7 has 2 cottage rooms + dormer capacity, plus
  the existing Bamboo Cafe seating. Effective ceiling: ~10 pax on a
  given weekend.
- **Events.** Z9 / Z7's outdoor deck; max ~ 40 pax seated, ~ 80
  standing.

If a metric exceeds **80%** of these ceilings for more than a month,
it is a signal to look at a sister-site expansion, not a queue.

## 6. Mapping to `07_DATA_MODEL.md`

These zone IDs map directly to `Zone.id` in the data model:

```
'Z1-greens-salad',     // Z1  Greens & salad beds
'Z2-roots-tubers',     // Z2  Root & tuber beds
'Z3-millets-pulses',   // Z3  Millets & pulses  ← identity crop
'Z4-gau-shala',        // Z4  Gau Shala + fodder
'Z5-pollinator-strip', // Z5  Pollinator & pest-trap strip
'Z6-apiary',           // Z6  Apiary
'Z7-homestead',        // Z7  Homestead + stay + café
'Z8-cow-shed-homa',    // Z8  Cow shed + Homa corner
'Z9-compost-nursery',  // Z9  Compost & nursery yard
'Z10-buffer',          // Z10 Buffer / wildlife + paths
```

Z4, Z6, Z8 (and parts of Z10) are **not** zones a subscriber gets
allocated to (they are inputs, not harvest). They appear on the
`/zones` map for transparency — *not* for sale.
