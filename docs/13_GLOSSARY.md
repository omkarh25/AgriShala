# 13 — Glossary

> Reference for terms that show up in these docs and in the brand voice.
> Each entry has a short definition, and where useful, the language of
> origin and the way the term is used on the platform.

## A

**A2 milk.** Milk from cows with the A2A2 beta-casein genotype, common
in Indian breeds (Malnad Gidda, Hallikar, Gir). Promoted on the
platform as the default — we test the herd, not the breed.

**Agnihotra.** A Vedic fire offering performed at sunrise and sunset
in a copper pyramid with cow-dung cakes, ghee, rice, and **samidhas**.
We treat it as an *integrated atmospheric and pest-management practice*,
not a panacea or a marketing ritual. See `10_FARM_OPERATIONS.md` §2.

**Amaranth.** *Amaranthus spp.* A leafy / grain crop. Both *chaulai*
greens and grain amaranth are grown on the seed farm; greens appear in
the subscriber basket in kharif and summer.

**Apis cerana indica.** The Indian hive bee. The seed-farm apiary
hosts this species, not the European *Apis mellifera*. More disease-
resistant, less honey per hive, but better suited to local florals.

## B

**Bamboo Cafe.** Jayapal's organic cafe at Lokkanahalli. Pre-existed
AgriShala. Anchors the platform's on-site kitchen; in Phase 3 it
becomes the in-house kitchen for value-addition SKUs.

**Beejamrutha.** A seed-coating solution (cow-dung + cow-urine + lime
+ water) used before sowing. Part of the bio-fertilizer stack in
`10_FARM_OPERATIONS.md` §3.

**BRT Tiger Reserve.** **Biligiri Rangaswamy Temple Tiger Reserve** in
Karnataka, ~ 1,300 km² of dry-deciduous forest at the meeting of the
Western and Eastern Ghats. The seed farm sits on its edge.

## C

**Chamundihill.** A small hill shrine near Mysore often visited by
the BRT-edge farmers. We do not run rituals here — we may visit as
a guest excursion (Phase 2). Mentioned here because it often comes
up in conversation around the seed farm.

**Crop phase.** A tag on `PhotoUpdate`: `sow` / `grow` / `harvest` /
`pack`. Defined in `07_DATA_MODEL.md`.

**Community Marketplace.** A small marketplace that the platform
hosts on behalf of the surrounding indigenous community — Soliga-led
at Lokkanahalli. **Adjacent to** Sustenance, not part of it. The
community owns pricing and the audit; the platform keeps the rails
and charges a small fee. See `01_VISION.md §3a`,
`03_SEED_FARM.md §5`, `04_BUSINESS_MODEL.md §4a`. **Not the same
thing as** the "no marketplace" rule, which is about *our own*
retail.

## D

**Dapoli.** *Not* used here. We mention this only to say: don't confuse
with the **Dapoli Nashik method** of Homa farming in Maharashtra — the
seed farm's approach is informed by, but not a copy of, those farms.

## F

**Foxtail millet.** *Setaria italica.* A small millet, kharif-grown in
Z3. Used in `04_BUSINESS_MODEL.md` retail catalogue.

## G

**Gau Shala.** Literally *cow-shelter*. In the AgriShala context it is
the **cow-integration** practice: the herd (5 animals, see
`10_FARM_OPERATIONS.md` §1) is the centre of the nutrient cycle (dung
+ urine + milk + bull-power).

**Gobar.** Cow-dung. The base of practically every bio-fertilizer on
the farm (compost, gobar slurry, Agnihotra cakes, Jeevamrutha).

## H

**Hallikar.** A South-Indian draught cattle breed, native to Karnataka.
One of two breed candidates for the herd (alongside Malnad Gidda);
decision tracked as **Q-14**.

**Homa.** A general Sanskrit word for *fire offering*. We use it as an
umbrella term for Agnihotra and the supporting fire practices. Always
with a lowercase *h* in copy.

**Horsegram.** *Macrotyloma uniflorum.* A hardy pulse, rabi-grown in
Z3; nutrition-dense and drought-tolerant.

## J

**Jeevamrutha.** A fermented soil-drench made from cow-dung, cow-urine,
jaggery, pulse flour, and a handful of soil. Applied every two weeks
in the growing season.

**Jenumutti.** A small hill shrine in the BRT valley, visible from the
seed farm. An excursion anchor; we don't farm there.

**Jowar.** *Sorghum bicolor.* A rabi-grown millet, the second pillar
of Z3 after ragi.

## K

**Kharif.** The south-west monsoon cropping season (roughly Jun–Sep).
The platform's primary sowing season. Distinguished from **rabi**
(Oct–Jan) and **zaid** (Feb–May, summer).

**KVK.** Krishi Vigyan Kendra — the Indian Council of Agricultural
Research's district-level farm-science centres. We send soil samples
to the nearest KVK for `YieldRecord` audits (Phase 3).

## L

**Lokkanahalli.** The village where the seed farm sits. On the edge of
the BRT Tiger Reserve, ~3.5 hours by road from Bengaluru. Tagged in
all external comms as the platform's address.

## M

**Malnad Gidda.** A small, hardy dwarf-cattle breed native to the
Malnad region of Karnataka. Herd-breed candidate; see **Q-14**.

**Moringa / drumstick.** *Moringa oleifera.* A perennial vegetable tree
whose pods and leaves appear in the subscriber basket in summer.

## P

**Panchagavya.** A fermented foliar spray made from five cow products
(dung, urine, milk, curd, ghee) plus jaggery, banana, and coconut
water. Applied at 3% dilution.

**Polytunnel.** A simple plastic-clad tunnel used for nursery beds in
the monsoon. We do not invest in them in Phase 1 — natural shade cloth
is enough.

## Q

**Q-NN.** Marker for items in `14_OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`. Every `Q-NN` in
the docs refers back to that single list.

## R

**Rabi.** The post-monsoon winter cropping season (Oct–Jan). The
second sowing season of the year.

**Ragi.** *Eleusine coracana.* Finger millet. The identity crop of
the seed farm, kharif-grown in Z3. The retail-SKU flagship.

## S

**Samidha.** A specific dried twig (often *palasha* / *butea* /
*shami*) used as fuel in the Agnihotra offering. Stocked once a year.

**Sesbania.** *Sesbania grandiflora / sesban.* A fast-growing
nitrogen-fixing fodder tree, planted on the Z4 perimeter.

**Shami.** *Not used.* Mentioned here to disambiguate from another
Homa-school's terminology. Our samidhas are sourced by one named
supplier — see `10_FARM_OPERATIONS.md` §2.

**Soliga.** A Soliga Adivasi community that has farmed the BRT
landscape for centuries. The seed farm is a partner, not a patron:
we learn from them; we pay fair rates; we hire from the surrounding
villages whenever possible.

## T

**Tamarind.** *Tamarindus indica.* A long-cycle native tree that
appears as both a shade element in Z10 and a flavour on the platform
(retail SKU: tamarind pickle in Phase 3).

**Tibetan settlement.** A small Tibetan diaspora community living
near the seed farm. An excursion anchor (`FR-2.3`). Not a sales
target.

**Toor / Tur / Arhar.** *Cajanus cajan.* A pigeon-pea pulse,
kharif-grown in Z3; the second-most important pulse after
horsegram.

## U

**ULID.** Universally Lexicographically Sortable Identifier. We use
ULIDs as primary keys throughout `07_DATA_MODEL.md`. They are sortable
strings and a small advantage over UUIDs.

## V

**Vertical integration.** The practice of taking the raw produce all
the way to a finished retail SKU (ghee from milk, soap from milk,
pickles from raw mango). Pillar 3 of the platform.

## W

**Wendell Berry.** *Reference author*. The voice rulebook in
`08_CONTENT_STRATEGY.md` is partly modelled on his essays — concrete,
place-rooted, suspicious of convenience.

## Y

**YieldRecord.** A Phase-3 entity (`07_DATA_MODEL.md`) tracking actual
weekly harvest weight per zone + crop. Used for the *yearly soil
report* and for capacity re-planning.

## Z

**Z1 … Z10.** The ten zone IDs in `11_FARM_ZONING.md`. Used as
literal `Zone.id` strings in the data model. Zones are not "rows";
they are **named and bounded patches of land**.

**Zaid.** The summer cropping season (Feb–May). The third (and
smallest) sowing window of the year.
