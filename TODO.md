Let food be your medicine.

Agrishala is a wholistic simplification of a consumer with his food.

Subscription:
1 acre land is divided into zones and consumers can subcribe to part of the farm for weekly door delivery service. The subscribers are sent periodic photo updates from their part of farm before delivered on a specified day to their home.

Experience:
The farm usually contains space to stay over the weekend and use the facilities for their events etc., It contains stay, local excursion, cooking etc.,

Sustenance:
The farm also maximizes self sustainability by vertical integration in final products for wider market consumption. This is the larger picture of how to zone the farms and what kinda practices and guidelines for long term sustenance. Gau shala, Agnihotra method etc.,
